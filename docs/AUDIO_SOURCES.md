# JoTrip DMC - real sound recording candidates (not yet shipped)

Reviewed 2026-09-30 for possible Phase 6 licensing and sound design.

- Mixkit Sea: https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/sea/ . Candidate: **Distant sea humming ambiance** (0:44) or **Sea waves loop** (0:48). Prefer the former for a quiet room; listen and verify the actual individual downloaded item before release.
- Mixkit Paper: https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/paper/ . Candidate: **Page turn single** (0:01), **Single book paging** (0:02). Avoid metallic game chimes and heavy effects.
- Mixkit Free SFX License: https://mixkit.co/license/modal/sfxFree/ . Allows sound effects in commercial end products including public websites, but forbids redistribution standalone, as stock, in a tool or template or with source files; retain proof of license and don't ship files unreviewed.

No sound files have been downloaded or shipped in this phase; until individual assets are selected, listened to and recorded with exact download URLs/license evidence, the visible sound button remains clearly disabled. This is deliberate: do not substitute fake synthesised sounds or autoplay.

Target mix: far-away sea <= -30 dBFS perceived level, extremely gentle real page rustle, fade in/out, no autoplay, option to disable, tab visibility respects silence. Serve compressed audio from same origin only after explicit user opt-in. Downloaded SFX licenses are not an authorization to redistribute isolated source audio in this public repo; confirm final distribution terms before committing.
