GUEST HANDLING

Local does not mean casual.

Warmth and operational discipline should coexist. The guest can feel relaxed because the people behind the day are paying attention to the details that matter.

  • Clear named local responsibility
  • Guest preferences passed through handovers
  • Timing and movement checked against reality
  • Plan changes explained with useful options
  • No invented certainty when conditions are unclear
