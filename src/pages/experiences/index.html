01 · SEA

The sea is not
a backdrop.

It decides timing, route, comfort and sometimes whether the original plan should happen at all. Private water days are designed with conditions first, then the guest.

SEA · PRIVATE WATER

A day that can still change its mind.

Some guests want quiet coves. Some want a long lunch. Some want to fish seriously. Some want to be back before the children run out of energy. A private sea day should be able to respond to all of that.

  • Private boat and crew selection
  • Route shaped around operating conditions
  • Family pace and comfort considered
  • Food, equipment and shore options coordinated
02 · ROOTS

Culture works better when it stays human.

Fish sauce, pepper, fruit, markets and local tables can become empty stops if nobody explains the people and logic behind them. We prefer fewer places, more context and time to actually notice.

  • Fish sauce houses and local producers
  • Pepper, fruit and food stories
  • Home-style and carefully chosen tables
  • Hosts who can translate context, not scripts
03 · WILD

Further out needs better judgement.

Sport fishing, diving and nature-led days depend on equipment, crew, weather, sea and the guest's actual experience. Luxury here is not decoration. It is preparation and the confidence to modify the plan.

  • Big-game and sport fishing
  • Diving and marine experiences
  • Nature and conservation-led days
  • Clear go, hold, modify or cancel judgement
04 · MOMENTS

The trip belongs to the people in it.

A celebration, a family holiday or simply one unhurried afternoon should not feel like a production. We plan the hidden pieces so the visible moment stays natural.

  • Multi-generation family journeys
  • Private meals and celebrations
  • Couples and quiet time
  • Flexible days with fewer handovers

JOURNEY SHAPES

Different people need
different structures.

These are not products with fixed inclusions. They are operating shapes JoTrip can compose around the guest, hotel, dates and reason for travelling.

Private island day

One boat, one host and a day shaped around pace, food, sea and the option to change course.

02

Island roots journey

Producers, food and local stories connected into a meaningful half or full day.

03

Fishing day

Serious fishing logic, equipment and conditions rather than a decorative activity added to a tour.

04

Family rhythm

Less transfer, more recovery time and choices that work for several generations at once.

05

Stay-led journey

The resort is part of the experience, with island days placed around the value of the stay rather than competing with it.
